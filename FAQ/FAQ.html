Before you do, have a quick look at our extensive FAQ

Why do you only offer set sizes?

We use standard sizes that suit most needs and help us keep prices affordable.

Do you frame existing artwork?

No. We focus on printing, framing, and delivering new artwork.

Is my file big enough?

Our uploader checks your file quality and only enables sizes that will print clearly.

What is a giclée print?

A giclée print is a high quality fine art print made using archival inks and paper. It is the industry standard for galleries and museums and is designed to last for decades without fading.

What type of printer do you use?

We use professional Epson printer with genuine archival inks to produce consistent, gallery quality results.

What type of paper do you use?

We print on 310 gsm 100 percent cotton rag paper as our standard, chosen for its durability, texture, and archival quality.

Do you use real timber moulding?

Yes. We use genuine timber sourced locally from sustainable suppliers, including Victorian Ash and Tasmanian Oak.

Are the frames mass produced?

No. Every frame is hand cut and assembled by our team in our Burleigh Heads workshop.

How long will delivery take?

Your order will arrive within approximately 2-3 weeks.

Where is my order shipped from?

All orders are shipped from our workshop in Burleigh Heads on the Gold Coast.

How much does shipping cost?

We offer Australia wide shipping. Costs may vary depending on your order.

How are the frames shipped?

Frames are carefully bubble wrapped and packed in custom made, double wall cardboard boxes. All shipments are insured against loss or damage.

How can I track my order?

You will receive an email with your tracking number once your order has been dispatched, typically 10 to 12 days after placing your order.

How can I change my address after purchasing?

You can contact us to update your address as long as your order has not yet been shipped. After dispatch, you can contact the courier directly using the details provided in your tracking email.
