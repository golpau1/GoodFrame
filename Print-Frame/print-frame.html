Photo quality requirements

Our quality checker reviews the resolution of your uploaded photo and shows which print sizes will produce a clear result.

Larger prints need more image detail. Cropping or zooming into your photo reduces the available resolution, so some larger sizes may become unavailable.

Minimum resolution at 160 PPI

  • Small — 210 × 297 mm: 1323 × 1871 px
  • Medium — 420 × 594 mm: 2646 × 3742 px
  • Large — 594 × 841 mm: 3742 × 5298 px
  • Extra-large — 841 × 1189 mm: 5298 × 7490 px

For the best results, upload the original full-resolution photo rather than a screenshot or an image downloaded from social media.
