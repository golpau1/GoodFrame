FAQ

How many pictures can I upload?

You can upload up to eight pictures for the picture frame option.

What size are the frames?

Each frame is 80 × 80 mm and designed to hold one of your favourite photos.

What image files can I upload?

PNG, JPEG, and GIF image files are supported.

Can I order the frame without pictures?

Yes. Choose “Frame Only” before adding it to your cart.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery timing will be confirmed with your order details.

Can I change my pictures after ordering?

Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to make a change.
